Intentional evidence-semantic hardening
---------------------------------------
The original v1.1 acceptance test expected an unsigned acquisition metadata
entry carrying `authoritative:true` to promote evidence to AUTHORITATIVE.

v1.2 intentionally rejects that promotion.

New behavior:
- unsigned authoritative claim + valid artifact/hash -> at most VALIDATED
- valid signed acquisition manifest + verified artifact hashes -> may promote to
  AUTHORITATIVE/CORRELATED

This is an intentional forensic correctness change.
