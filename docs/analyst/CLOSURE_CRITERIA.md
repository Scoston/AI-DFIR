# Case Closure Criteria

Before closure confirm:

- mandatory Evidence Pack requirements meet required quality or are explicitly
  documented as unavailable/waived under approved policy;
- critical identity/trust findings are resolved;
- provider/collector coverage gaps are understood;
- open delegated work is zero or formally transferred;
- target-system consequences are reconciled;
- containment/recovery state is understood;
- repository/evidence integrity checks pass;
- legal holds remain enforced or have a valid signed release;
- peer review is complete for material conclusions;
- unsupported conclusions are not stated as facts.

Deployment readiness failure is operational risk, not proof that an incident did
or did not occur; record it separately from incident findings.
